### Week 6 Objectives

* Learn managed relational databases with Amazon RDS.
* Store and retrieve sensitive configuration securely with AWS Secrets Manager.

| Date | Task | Reference material |
| --- | --- | --- |

### Week 6 Achievements

* Understood the benefits of managed relational databases with Amazon RDS.
* Learned how VPC networking and security groups protect database access.
* Understood why credentials should be stored in Secrets Manager rather than application code.
* Learned the basic workflow for allowing an application to retrieve a secret using IAM permissions.
