### Overall Evaluation

**1. Learning and Working Environment**
I highly value the open learning environment at AWS FCAJ. The content is organized into a clear learning path, which helped me approach AWS services from foundational to more advanced topics. Combining learning with hands-on practice and projects also encouraged me to explore technology proactively.

**2. Support from Mentors and the Admin Team**
The mentors and admin team provided effective support, learning materials, and guidance whenever I encountered difficulties. I especially appreciated the approach of encouraging members to research, experiment, and attempt to solve problems before receiving detailed guidance.

**3. Relevance of the Program Content**
The program content aligns well with my cloud-computing learning goals. Topics such as IAM, EC2, S3, VPC, ECS Fargate, RDS, CloudWatch, and AWS CLI are connected logically, helping me understand how services work together in a practical application architecture.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**
The program improved my ability to read documentation, configure cloud resources, deploy applications, monitor systems, and troubleshoot basic issues. Learning and working on projects also helped me develop proactiveness, problem-solving, and technical-presentation skills.

### Suggestions for Improvement

* Provide more complete architecture examples or sample projects to make the connections between AWS services easier to understand.
* Publish a weekly checklist or set of objectives so members can track their learning progress more easily.
